## Background  
I hold a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Texas Christian University (2007), and spent over 13 years in oil & gas leading complex field projects across XTO Energy, EnLink Midstream, and PERC Engineering. I designed and operated 500+ miles of pipeline and 50+ compressor stations across the Permian, Barnett, Delaware, and STACK basins—delivering urban relocations, process optimizations (amine, dehydration), and field-standardized station designs. At PERC, I led 220+ miles of new infrastructure and earned repeat client business through engineering precision and direct field collaboration.

Since 2021, I’ve served as CFO of Luna Therapy PLLC, a company I co-founded with my wife, where I doubled revenue through data-informed financial strategies. Today, I’m applying that same analytical rigor to build machine learning pipelines that forecast production, model well performance, and enable smarter field development decisions.

This project gives mineral rights holders access to public data—but with the kind of modeling, forecasting, and decision support tools traditionally reserved for producers. By analyzing tens of thousands of wells, applying time series forecasting, and visualizing performance by operator and lateral length, the platform unlocks insights on ultimate recovery, development strategy, and asset value.

It’s about empowering stakeholders to make data-backed decisions with full-field awareness, even without the in-house resources of an operator—bridging the gap between public transparency and private performance intelligence.
